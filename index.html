QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

A few helpful details about using Finora.

Who can use Finora?

Finora is designed as a starting point for students, farmers, shop owners, artisans, freelancers, workers, and other livelihood profiles.

Does a scheme match mean I am eligible?

No. Scheme matches and document lists are preparation aids. Confirm current eligibility and requirements with the official scheme portal or administering institution.

Does Finora connect to my bank or UPI account?

No. Finora has no live bank or UPI connection. You can review a supported text-based UPI statement PDF or sample data in the browser.

What kind of statement PDF can I import?

The current reader works with PDFs that contain selectable text. Scanned image-only statements are not supported. Review imported rows because formats can vary.

Are suspicious-activity flags definite proof of fraud?

No. Flags are rule-based prompts to review an entry or message. They can miss issues or flag harmless activity. Verify concerns through official channels and never share PINs or OTPs.

Do livelihood suggestions predict my income?

No. Suggestions use the records you enter to offer practical bookkeeping ideas. Revenue and gross margin are summaries of saved sales, not forecasts or guarantees.
